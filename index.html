How it works

Not a chatbot. An investigator.

1

You talk. It listens.

Type it out or say it out loud, whenever something's actually on your mind. No prompts, no forms to fill in.

2

It notices what repeats

It builds a picture of you, forming hypotheses from what you say and what it observes, then checking them against what actually happens next.

3

It gives you something to try

One small, concrete ask to test the theory. Then an honest look at what happened, including when it got things wrong.
