Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Zare ("Zare," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with the Zare mobile application (the "App" or the "Service"). This Policy applies to all users of the Service and is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Use. By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Service.

Who Operates Zare

Zare is operated as a sole proprietorship. For purposes of applicable data protection law, the operator of Zare is the entity responsible for determining how and why your information is processed, sometimes referred to as the "data controller" or "business." You can reach us at any time using the contact information at the end of this Policy.

Eligibility and Children's Privacy

The Service is intended solely for individuals who are at least sixteen (16) years of age. We are not directed to children under the age of thirteen (13), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under thirteen. Individuals between the ages of sixteen and eighteen may use the Service subject to any additional consent or supervision requirements imposed by the laws of their jurisdiction. We verify eligibility by asking for your date of birth during account creation, and we will not allow an account to be created if the date of birth provided indicates the person is under sixteen. If we later obtain actual knowledge that we have collected personal information from a person under sixteen, or under thirteen in a manner inconsistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), we will take reasonable steps to promptly delete that information and close the associated account.

Information We Collect

We collect the categories of information described below, directly from you, automatically through your use of the Service, and, in limited cases, from third parties such as Google when you choose to authenticate through them.

Account and Profile Information

When you create an account, we collect your first name, last name, and any nickname you provide; your email address, or the name and email address associated with your Google account if you register using "Continue with Google"; your date of birth, which we use to confirm your eligibility to use the Service and, where applicable, to personalize your experience; your gender, if you choose to provide it, which you may decline to share by selecting "prefer not to say"; your phone number, if you choose to provide one; and your device's time zone, which we use to schedule notifications and reviews at appropriate local times.

Conversation Content and Attachments

We collect and store the full content of your conversations with Zare, including every message you send and every response Zare generates. If you choose to attach a photograph to a conversation, the image data itself is transmitted to our artificial intelligence service provider so that it can be incorporated into Zare's response. If you attach a non-image file, such as a document, we record the file's name only; we do not extract, read, transmit, or otherwise process the contents of that file.

Voice and Speech Data

If you choose to compose a message using your device's voice input feature, your spoken words are converted into text using speech recognition functionality built into your device's operating system before that data ever reaches our servers. We receive and store only the resulting text, in the same manner as a typed message. We do not receive, transmit, or retain an audio recording of your voice.

AI-Generated Behavioral Data

A core function of the Service is to analyze your conversations over time and generate an evolving model of your behavior, habits, and motivations. This analysis produces several categories of information that we store on your behalf, including discrete facts Zare has learned about you; observed behavior patterns; hypotheses Zare forms about the reasons behind your behavior, together with the evidence supporting them; small experiments Zare may suggest you try; short daily notes; and periodic written reviews summarizing recurring themes on a weekly, monthly, or yearly basis. We consider this category of information to be highly sensitive, comparable in nature to a personal diary, and we handle it with the same protections described throughout this Policy.

Safety and Crisis-Related Information

If the content of a conversation suggests that you may be experiencing a mental health crisis or considering self-harm, Zare records what happened as part of its ongoing memory of you, in the same manner as any other significant fact or episode you share, so that Zare does not lose track of something important to you. Separately, we also store a flag indicating that a gentle, general check-in is owed in a future conversation, along with the date and time it was created. This information is deliberately excluded from your daily notes and periodic reviews, and Zare is instructed never to raise the substance of the disclosure again on its own or to treat a positive check-in as an invitation to keep discussing it, but the underlying record is retained as memory rather than deleted. The Service is not a crisis intervention tool and is not monitored in real time by a human being; if you are in immediate danger, please contact emergency services or a crisis line, such as the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the United States, directly.

Reports and Support Communications

If you use the in-app feature to report a message as inaccurate or inappropriate, we collect and store the reported message, the reason you selected, any additional description you provide, and a limited number of preceding messages needed to understand the context of your report. If you contact us directly for customer support, we collect and store your email address and the content of your message so that we can respond to you. These records are reviewed solely by us for the purpose of resolving your report or inquiry and improving the Service, and are not shared with any other party.

Subscription and Transaction Information

If you subscribe to a paid tier of the Service, all payment processing is performed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card number or other financial account information. We use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management platform, to receive and record subscription-related events, such as the start of a free trial, the tier you have subscribed to, and your renewal date, so that we can provision the correct level of access to your account.

Automatically Collected Technical and Usage Information

We and our service providers automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you use the Service, including the screens you view, in-app events such as completing onboarding, general device and operating system information, application version, crash and error diagnostic reports, your push notification token if notifications are enabled, and aggregate records of the computational resources used to process your conversations. This information relates to the operation and performance of the Service itself and does not include the substantive content of your conversations.

On-Device and Local Storage

In addition to the information we store on our servers, the App stores certain information locally on your device, such as your session credentials in your device's secure keychain or equivalent secure storage, and cached app data used to improve performance and allow the App to function while data is loading. This locally stored information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us except as otherwise described in this Policy. Certain data may also be encrypted on your device before being stored or transmitted, as an additional layer of protection.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to create, maintain, and secure your account; generate Zare's conversational responses and the behavioral insights, patterns, experiments, daily notes, and reviews described above; personalize the timing and content of notifications and check-ins; verify your eligibility to use the Service; enforce free-tier usage limits and manage paid subscriptions; provide customer support and investigate reports you submit; detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Use; diagnose and fix technical problems with the Service; and comply with applicable law, legal process, and government requests. We do not use the content of your conversations to build advertising profiles, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models operated by us or by our service providers.

Automated Processing and Profiling

The facts, patterns, hypotheses, experiments, and reviews described above are generated through automated analysis of your conversations. We do not use this automated processing to make any decision that produces a legal effect concerning you or that similarly significantly affects you, such as a decision about eligibility for a benefit, employment, credit, housing, insurance, or similar opportunity. The output of this automated processing is provided to you for your own reflection and is not shared with, or used to make decisions on behalf of, any third party. Where applicable law grants you rights with respect to automated decision-making or profiling, you may exercise those rights using the contact information at the end of this Policy.

Legal Bases for Processing (International Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that requires us to identify a legal basis for processing personal information, we process your information on the following bases, as applicable: performance of a contract with you, in order to provide the Service you have requested; our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Service, provided those interests are not outweighed by your rights; your consent, where we have specifically requested it, such as for optional features; and compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only to the following categories of recipients, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform their function.

Anthropic, the developer of the Claude family of artificial intelligence models, receives the text of your conversations and any photographs you attach in order to generate Zare's conversational responses, periodic reviews, and proactive check-in messages. Under Anthropic's commercial terms of service, content submitted through its business application programming interface is not used to train Anthropic's models.

Supabase provides the database, authentication, and backend infrastructure on which substantially all of your account and conversation data is hosted and processed.

Apple processes subscription payments through the App Store, delivers push notifications to your device, and, where you use voice input, performs on-device speech-to-text conversion.

Google receives and provides to us your name and email address if you choose to create or access your account using "Continue with Google."

RevenueCat receives subscription and purchase event data from Apple's App Store in order to help us track and manage your subscription status.

Sentry receives technical error and crash diagnostic information, such as which function of the Service failed and the associated error code, to help us identify and fix defects. Our integration with Sentry is configured so that the content of your conversations is not transmitted to it.

We may also disclose information where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms of Use; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Zare, our users, or the public, including in an emergency involving a risk to life; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, in which case information you have provided may be among the assets transferred, subject to the terms of this Policy or a materially similar successor policy. We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you, for any lawful business purpose.

International Data Transfers

The Service is operated from, and our service providers process information in, the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand and agree that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States and potentially other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those of your home jurisdiction. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer, storage, and processing.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active, because the value of the Service depends on Zare's ability to remember your history. If you delete your account through Settings, we permanently delete your account and substantially all associated information, including your messages, facts, patterns, hypotheses, experiments, daily notes, reviews, reports, and notification tokens, immediately upon confirmation, rather than after a waiting period. Notwithstanding the foregoing, we may retain a minimal, de-identified record of past purchase transactions for as long as necessary to comply with our accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations, and residual copies of deleted information may persist for a limited period in routine encrypted backups until those backups are rotated out of service in the ordinary course.
